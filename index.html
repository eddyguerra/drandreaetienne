Meet Dr. Andrea

Credentials establish authority. Story establishes connection.

Dr. Andrea Etienne, Psy.D., is an educator and author with years of leadership in special educational needs, behavioural strategy and professional training. She has sat with children who learn differently, with the parents and teachers who love them, and with adults still carrying the way they were once seen.

Across her practice, her writing and her conversations, she brings one idea to the centre: how we see ourselves shapes how we live, and that reflection can change.

The

Mirror Theory™

How we see ourselves shapes how we live.

The Mirror Theory explores how our experiences, relationships, environments and internal beliefs shape the way we see ourselves, and how changing that reflection can change the way we live.

An evolving framework. The Mirror Theory is Dr. Etienne's original conceptual framework. It is not yet a scientifically validated psychological theory; research and publication pathways are being developed.
